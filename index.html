About Us

Style Studio

Welcome to Style Studio, situated at The Junction Mall, Ground Floor, Room 32, with an alternative studio location at Westgate Mall, Westlands. We chose these prime locations for the convenience of our clients and to provide easy access across the city.

At Style Studio, our team of over twenty-five certified professionals (including 15 expert stylists and 10 master barbers) are dedicated to delivering an unmatched salon experience. We believe that your time is valuable. That is why our seamless online appointment system ensures you never have to wait in line.

Whether you need a precision haircut, dreadlock care, coloring, beard trimming, or relaxing spa treatments, you are guaranteed to walk out of Style Studio looking and feeling your absolute best. We can comfortably attend to multiple clients simultaneously with dedicated attention to detail.

Style Studio

Our professional studio provides an extensive collection of specialized treatments and services. Each session utilizes premium organic styling products and modern salon chairs designed for utmost comfort. Quality, precision, and personalized attention define the Style Studio experience.
